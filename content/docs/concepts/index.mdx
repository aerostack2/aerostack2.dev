These pages explain how Aerostack2 works, from the overall architecture down to each part of a
drone's stack. They're worth reading before you write a mission for a real drone, add support for a
new one or write your own plugins.

When a mission flies a drone, every part described here takes a turn. The mission starts a behavior.
The behavior sends motion references to the motion controller. The controller turns them into
commands for the aerial platform, which flies the drone and reports its sensors. The state estimator
turns those sensors into the drone's position, and the controller and behaviors use it to close the
loop.
