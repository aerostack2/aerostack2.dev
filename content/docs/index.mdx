---
title: Introduction
description: Aerostack2 is an open source ROS 2 framework for building autonomous aerial systems, from a single drone to a swarm.
icon: BookOpen
---

Aerostack2 gives you the parts of an autonomous drone that every project needs: the link to the
hardware, state estimation, motion control and a library of behaviors such as take off, go to and
land. You write the mission on top, in Python or as a behavior tree, and the framework does the
rest.

- **Hardware-independent.** Your mission talks to a standard set of ROS 2 interfaces. Moving from
  simulation to a real Crazyflie, Tello, DJI or PX4 drone means changing the aerial platform, not the
  mission.
- **Modular.** Each component is a ROS 2 node, and most of them load their algorithm as a plugin, so
  you can swap a controller or an estimator without touching the rest.
- **Multi-robot.** Each drone runs its own copy of the stack under its own namespace, so one drone
  and a swarm are built the same way.
- **Open source.** Released under the [BSD 3-Clause license](/docs/about/license) and open to anyone
  who wants to take part.
