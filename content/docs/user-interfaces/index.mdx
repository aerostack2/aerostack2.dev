Aerostack2 ships with tools for flying a drone by hand and for watching what it's doing. They talk to
the same topics, services and behaviors as a mission, so they can run alongside one, for example to
watch a mission fly or to pause it.

| Tool | What it's for | Drones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Keyboard teleoperation](/docs/user-interfaces/keyboard-teleoperation) | Take off, move and land from the keyboard, and pause or resume running behaviors | One or many |
| [Alphanumeric viewer](/docs/user-interfaces/alphanumeric-viewer) | Read a drone's sensors, pose, references and modes in a terminal | One |
| [Visualization](/docs/user-interfaces/visualization) | See the drone, its trail and its reference in RViz, with a panel for take-off, landing and the kill switch | One or many |

Every tool takes the drone's namespace, and in simulation `use_sim_time`.
