This section is for working on Aerostack2 itself, whether that's fixing a bug, contributing a
feature, or writing a component of your own for your drone or research.

Aerostack2 is made to be extended. Most of what a drone does comes from plugins and nodes built on a
few base classes, so you can add a new platform, controller, estimator or behavior without touching
the rest of the stack. The tutorials walk through each of them.
