## The project

INSERTION advances the technology that teams of robots need to inspect and
maintain installations in harsh environments safely. The teams mix aerial,
ground and surface vehicles. The project develops localization for scenes with
poor visibility, navigation through clutter, cooperation between the three
kinds of vehicle, and control that keeps aerial and surface vehicles steady
under disturbances.

Robots in these places take people out of hazardous work and improve working
conditions. Target applications include sewers, offshore oil and gas platforms,
wind turbines, gas and power tunnels, mines, industrial storage tanks and
polluted water bodies.

The project has three global objectives.

- **Perception in harsh environments.** Robust localization, perception and
  mapping that keep working in fog, rain or darkness, by combining cameras,
  LiDAR and radar.
- **Navigation and control in complex environments.** Control strategies for
  aerial and surface vehicles in wind gusts, water currents and strong
  disturbances, for safe autonomous navigation through 3D clutter.
- **Multi-robot cooperation.** Cooperation between aerial, ground and surface
  vehicles, from loose swarm-based cooperation to tight cooperation between
  robots joined by a tether.

## Sub-projects

INSERTION is a coordinated project with three sub-projects.

1. **Robust localization, mapping and planning in harsh environments**, led by
   the [Service Robotics Laboratory](https://www.upo.es/robot/en/) of the
   Universidad Pablo de Olavide. It covers localization, mapping and planning
   for heterogeneous robots, and their coordination.
2. **UAV perception, control and operation in harsh environments**, led by
   [CVAR](https://cvar.fi.upm.es/) at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. It
   brings autonomy for single drones and drone teams in GPS-denied areas.
3. **Cooperation of USVs and UAVs for inspection in dynamic environments**, led
   by [ISCAR](https://www.iscar.es/) at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid.
   It brings surface vehicles, cooperation between heterogeneous robots, and
   applications in water.

INSERTION builds on COMCISE, an earlier coordinated project on inspection and
security missions by drones working with ground robots (RTI2018-100847-C11 and
C12). It widens that scope to surface vehicles, harsher environments with
poorer visibility, and more complex cooperation between robots.

## Aerostack2 in INSERTION

Both CVAR-UPM and the Service Robotics Laboratory at UPO use Aerostack2 to fly
the project's aerial fleets, which coordinate with surface vehicles in
inspection scenarios. CVAR-UPM's missions for the project are in
[project_insertion](https://github.com/cvar-upm/project_insertion).
