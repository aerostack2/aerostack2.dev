COPILOT-CM brings drones, IoT and advanced communications together to monitor,
inspect and operate photovoltaic plants. It is a synergic R&D project of the
Comunidad de Madrid, coordinated by the GEISER group of the Universidad de
Alcalá with CVAR-UPM, which flies its drones with Aerostack2.

- **Funder.** Comunidad de Madrid, Proyectos Sinérgicos de I+D 2020
- **Reference.** Y2020/EMT-6368
- **Coordinator.** GEISER, Universidad de Alcalá
- **Links.** [Project website](https://geiser.depeca.uah.es/copilot/index.php/es/)

## The project

Its full title, in Spanish, is _Control, supervisión y operación optimizada de
plantas fotovoltaicas mediante integración sinérgica de drones, IoT y
tecnologías avanzadas de comunicaciones_. Large solar plants have thousands of
panels, and finding the faulty ones by hand is slow. COPILOT-CM fits each panel
with IoT electronics that report its state, and sends drones to inspect the
panels that need a closer look.

## Aerostack2 in COPILOT-CM

The inspection drones run Aerostack2 and work together with the IoT network on
the panels. The papers below describe the framework and how its missions moved
from simulation to real flights.
