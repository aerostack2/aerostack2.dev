SHEREC is a Horizon Europe innovation action that brings robots, data and AI
into ship recycling. CVAR-UPM is a partner and develops the localization and
navigation of its inspection drone on Aerostack2.

- **Programme.** Horizon Europe, call HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-CNECT
- **Grant agreement.** 101136056
- **Dates.** January 2024 to December 2027
- **Coordinator.** HIDROPAR, Türkiye
- **Links.** [sherecproject.com](https://www.sherecproject.com/) and
  [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101136056)

## The project

Ship recycling takes ships apart so their materials can be reused, which makes
it part of Europe's circular economy. It is also one of the most dangerous
jobs in the world, with high rates of injury and exposure to toxic waste.
SHEREC sets out to make it safer and cleaner in three ways.

1. An AI-powered drone inspects the inside of the ship and finds hazardous
   materials.
2. A digital twin of the ship and AI planning produce the recycling plan
   automatically.
3. Two mobile robots, autonomous or teleoperated, carry out the cutting and
   paint removal.

## Aerostack2 in SHEREC

The inspection drone flies inside ships, where there is no GPS. CVAR-UPM leads
its semantic SLAM, which keeps the drone's position free of drift by
recognising walls, rooms, doors and equipment as landmarks.

- [sherec_semantic_slam](https://github.com/cvar-upm/sherec_semantic_slam) is
  deliverable D2.4 of the project. It runs onboard as an Aerostack2 node and
  publishes the corrected pose that navigation uses, along with a semantic map
  of the ship's interior.
- [project_sherec_navigation](https://github.com/cvar-upm/project_sherec_navigation)
  is an example of the Aerostack2 navigation developed for the project, which
  runs in simulation with Docker.
