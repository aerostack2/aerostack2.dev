CoreSense is a Horizon Europe research and innovation action that develops a
cognitive architecture for autonomous robots, grounded in formal theories of
understanding and awareness. The Universidad Politécnica de Madrid coordinates
it, and CVAR-UPM builds its drone demonstrator on Aerostack2.

- **Programme.** Horizon Europe, call HORIZON-CL4-2021-DIGITAL-EMERGING-01
- **Grant agreement.** 101070254
- **Dates.** October 2022 to September 2026
- **Coordinator.** Universidad Politécnica de Madrid
- **Links.** [coresense.eu](https://coresense.eu) and
  [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101070254)

## The project

Robots now work in open environments that their designers can't fully foresee.
They often don't understand what is going on around them or their own role in
it, which makes them hard to rely on and hard to trust. CoreSense answers this
with a theory of understanding, a theory of awareness, reusable software that
applies both to real robots, and three demonstrations of what they bring. The
demonstrations make drone teams more resilient, manufacturing robots more
flexible, and social robots better aligned with the people around them.

## Aerostack2 in CoreSense

The drone demonstration is the
[inspection testbed](https://coresenseeu.github.io/testbeds/docs/inspection_testbed.html),
where a swarm of drones inspects an array of solar panels. The drones split the
area through an auction, fly their coverage lanes, and take over the waypoints
of a drone that fails mid-mission. Aerostack2 is the runtime of the whole
testbed, and its simulation runs entirely in the Aerostack2 multirotor
simulator.

- [TB2_Panel_Inspection_Simulation](https://github.com/CoreSenseEU/TB2_Panel_Inspection_Simulation)
  holds the simulated inspection missions, written with the Aerostack2 Python
  API.
- [as2_knowledge_graph](https://github.com/aerostack2/as2_knowledge_graph)
  is the knowledge graph behind the knowledge-driven missions listed below.
- The [simulated dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/23162059) and the
  [ROS bag dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/23243295) record the testbed's
  missions.
