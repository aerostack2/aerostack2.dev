Aerostack2 is developed by CVAR-UPM in the course of European and Spanish
research projects, and other groups build projects of their own on it. This
section covers the projects with public evidence of Aerostack2 in use, with the
papers and videos that came out of them.

## Used by others

Groups outside CVAR-UPM have built their own funded projects on Aerostack2.

- **GLITTER.** A Horizon Europe Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral network on
  satellite Earth observation, grant agreement 101120117. The SnT centre of the
  University of Luxembourg used Aerostack2 to simulate drone formations in
  Gazebo that stand in for SmallSat formations, in
  [Emulating SmallSat Missions With UAVs](https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2026-0177)
  at the AIAA SciTech 2026 Forum.
- **CROMU.** Climate Resilience by Operating Multi UAS, by
  [WPWEB](https://www.wpweb.com/ricerca-e-sviluppo/cromu/) in Italy and
  co-financed by the National Center for Sustainable Mobility in 2024 and 2025.
  Aerostack2 was the foundation of its multi-vehicle management system, a
  demonstrator that explores simulated territory to prevent and manage
  climate-related risks.
- **Universidad de Zaragoza.** The experiments in
  [CHORAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10340), a planner for safe and efficient
  routing of heterogeneous robot teams, run on Aerostack2.

If your project uses Aerostack2, tell us in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions) and
we'll add it here.
