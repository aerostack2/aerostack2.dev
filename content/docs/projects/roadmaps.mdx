## 1.2.0

The next release is tracked in the
[v1.2.0 milestone](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/milestone/2). Most
of it is already on main.

- **ROS 2 Jazzy.** Every package builds for Jazzy as well as Humble.
- **Pixi packages.** Aerostack2 installs with Pixi on Linux and macOS, and the
  packages are [published on prefix.dev](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/1021) with each release.
- **Motion controller.** A new [geometric controller](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/1001), and a
  [parameter contract](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/1000) that every controller plugin follows.
- **State estimator.** The estimator is
  [reworked so plugins no longer publish the state](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/980) themselves, and
  [simple_ekf](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/1002) adds a 15-state EKF.
- **Command frames.** A platform [declares the frames](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/990) its commands
  are in, and [direct command handlers](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/993) send commands straight to it.
- **Trajectory generation.** The trajectory generator is now a
  [plugin](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/941), with plugins for
  [mav_trajectory_generation](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/942), [GCOPTER](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/943) and a
  [jerk-limited generator](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/944).
- **Perception and payload.** A new
  [object perception package](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/910) with a modular pipeline, and
  [payload behaviors](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/900) such as pointing a gimbal.
- **Behaviors.** A behavior can [pause itself](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/pull/940).

## Under discussion

These ideas are open on
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/categories/ideas). None of them is
planned yet, so join in if one matters to you.

- [A new motion controller architecture](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/938), and
  [less duplicated code](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/927) between the motion controller and the
  multirotor simulator
- [A trajectory splines interface](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/936) in as2_msgs
- [Multiplatform support](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/920)
- [Path planning for fixed-wing aircraft](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/934) and the
  [future of the path planning behavior](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/922)
- [A more modular behavior tree](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/896)
- [Moving the CLI from Bash to Python](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/916)
- [WildBridge for DJI drones](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/913) and
  [support for the DJI Matrice 400](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions/966)
