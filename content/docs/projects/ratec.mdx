## The project

RATEC pairs a mobile ground robot with a drone connected to it by a power
cable. Tethered drones get around the short flight time of small aerial
vehicles, but the systems available so far are static, which limits them in
inspection and maintenance missions in complex places. RATEC takes the
prototype the team is developing a step closer to industry.

The ground robot and the drone work as a marsupial team, physically joined by a
cable that carries power and data. The drone becomes an extension of the ground
robot that reaches places the robot can't. The robot's batteries extend the
drone's flight time, and the cable lets the robot do the heavy computing. In
return, every joint manoeuvre has to respect the constraints of the cable,
especially in complex environments.

The project industrializes the subsystems developed in COMCISE and validates the
whole system in a relevant operational environment, raising it to technology
readiness level 7. The result is a proof of concept of a tethered team for
long inspection and maintenance missions, mainly where there is no GPS, such as
tunnels, sewers, underground galleries, large logistics warehouses, and search
and rescue after natural disasters. The ground robot carries the batteries,
sensors and computers to work on its own, and the drone flies from it on the
cable.
