Aerostack2 makes autonomous aerial robots easier to build and to use. Its
components talk over ROS 2, so you can improve one part of a system without
touching the rest. It doesn't depend on any one drone, which is what lets the
same mission move from simulation to real flight. That path has been taken
many times, in research and in industrial projects such as the inspection of
wind turbines and solar plants.

Aerostack2 is open source under the
[BSD 3-Clause license](/docs/about/license), and anyone who wants to take part
is welcome. [CVAR](https://cvar.fi.upm.es/), the Computer Vision and Aerial
Robotics group of the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, led by Pascual Campoy,
started the project and supports it heavily, but Aerostack2 doesn't belong to
the group alone. We want it to be a community where people share and build on
each other's work. Use it, change it, and add your own components.

## Contact

- **Questions and ideas.** Start a thread in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests.** Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2/issues).
- **Contributions.** Open a pull request on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/aerostack2/aerostack2). The
  [Develop](/docs/develop) section explains how.
- **Research collaborations with CVAR.** Reach the group through the
  [CVAR website](https://cvar.fi.upm.es/).
